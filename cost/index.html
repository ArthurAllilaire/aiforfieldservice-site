What AI for field service costs in 2026

By Arthur Allilaire, co-founder and CTO of Kenner · Vendor facts checked on 10 November 2026

The short answer

Three of the twelve vendors we checked publish a price, and Kenner, which we make, is free while in beta. The rest quote on request, and for most suite AI the real cost is the suite licence plus an add-on.

  • Salesforce Agentforce for Field Service: $125 per user per month, on top of Field Service licences.
  • Microsoft Copilot in Dynamics 365 Field Service: included in the $105 per user per month Field Service licence.
  • MaintainX Assist: plans from $0 to $65 per user per month. Which plans include Assist is not clear.
  • Kenner: free while in beta. Prices after the beta are not published. Our product.
  • IFS Cloud Field Service Management with IFS.ai: not published. Needs the IFS.ai Activation Pass or a token add-on.
  • SAP Field Service and Asset Management with Joule: not published. Most generative features are premium.

What the price does not include

The licence underneath

Suite AI only runs inside its suite. Salesforce lists the $125 separately from the Field Service licences themselves.

Add-ons and tiers

IFS needs an Activation Pass or tokens. SAP treats most of its generative features as premium. Microsoft's Copilot needs a paid Field Service environment and is not available in trials.

Set-up

A specialist has to be connected to your systems before it is useful. Ask who does that work and whether it is charged.

Questions to ask about price

  1. Is it priced per user, per technician, per asset or by usage?
  2. Which plan or add-on do the AI features need?
  3. Is there a pilot or trial, and what does it cost?
  4. What does integration with our systems cost, and who does it?

Questions and answers

Is there free AI for field service?

MaintainX has a free plan, though which plans include its AI is not clear. Aquant offers a free trial sign-up. Kenner is free while in beta and starts with a pilot.

Why do so few vendors publish prices?

Most are sold to large organisations by quote. The pages we checked give no reason, so we do not guess at one.
